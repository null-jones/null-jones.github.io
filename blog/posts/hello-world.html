Hello World

2026.04.06 Ellie Jones 2 min read

Welcome to my corner of the internet. I’ve been meaning to set up a proper space to write for a while now, and here it finally is.

A bit about me

I’m a data scientist and software developer based in Calgary, AB. By day I lead analytics at Wyvern, where we’re building hyperspectral satellite imagery products. By night (and most weekends), I’m either writing code or deep in some geospatial rabbit hole.

Why this site exists

This site is a place to share things I’m working on or thinking about. If you’ve found your way here, you might be interested in:

  • Geospatial data science and remote sensing
  • Creative coding and data visualization
  • The occasional deep-dive into a side project

About the contours

You might notice the contour lines throughout the site. They’re generated by Contourist, a tool I built for creating topographic joy plots from real elevation data.
